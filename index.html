FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does PixelHelm sell?

PixelHelm sells automated 24/7 conversion tracking assurance for marketing agencies. Instead of finding out days later that a client theme change broke your tracking, our automated bots test your client funnels continuously and alert you the second a pixel or GTM trigger fails.

Why do I need PixelHelm if my client's site is online?

Uptime tools (like Pingdom or Datadog) only check if the web server returns HTTP 200. They are completely blind to marketing scripts. If a button class is renamed, orders still process normally, but Meta and Google receive 0 conversion pings. The bidding algorithm assumes campaigns stopped working and diverts client ad budget toward low-intent traffic.

How is payment handled during automated tests?

No real credit cards are charged. Our synthetic runner uses your store's private 100% off QA discount code to complete a $0 order, or stops right at the payment step while verifying all prior DOM triggers. Orders are tagged so store owners can filter or auto-archive them without affecting sales numbers or inventory.

Will synthetic tests pollute our client's live ad attribution?

No. Our automated runner inspects the outgoing network requests directly from the browser without attaching live paid ad click IDs. We verify that the payload, price, and event structure are valid without artificially inflating your real campaign conversion counts.
