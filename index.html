FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why do I need this?
Paid media platforms use automated machine learning feedback to find buying customers for your campaigns. When a site theme or plugin update breaks a button selector, the customer can still buy, but no conversion signal reaches the ad network. Because the platform thinks conversions stopped, the bidding algorithm begins wasting your client budget on cheap, low-intent traffic. PixelHelm catches this immediately so you can fix the issue before campaign performance degrades.
How do I set up a funnel with the browser extension?
You install our Chrome extension, start recording, and complete a normal test checkout on your client site once. The extension records the URLs, input fields, and network pings sent during the process. After that, our automated cloud runners take over and repeat the check continuously without requiring further manual work.
Will synthetic tests pollute our client's live ad attribution?
No. Our automated runner inspects the outgoing network requests directly from the browser without attaching live paid ad click IDs. We verify that the payload, price, and event structure are valid without artificially inflating your real campaign conversion counts.
